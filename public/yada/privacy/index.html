Yada Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026. Applies to Yada Intervals, Yada Tuner, and Yada Metronome version 1.0.

Yada apps do not require an account. They contain no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. They do not send app data to the developer or an analytics service.

Local Storage

Your settings and saved content are stored on your device. Yada Intervals stores training sessions. Yada Tuner stores your selected tuning and appearance. Yada Metronome stores practice settings, appearance, and named presets. Device backups are governed by your Apple account and device settings.

Yada Tuner And Microphone Access

Yada Tuner requests microphone access to measure guitar pitch. Audio is processed in memory on your device. The app does not save recordings or upload audio. Microphone capture stops when the app is inactive. You can revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings.

Yada Intervals, Apple Health, And Apple Watch

Health tracking is optional. With your permission, Yada Intervals reads heart rate and active energy and saves workouts and active energy to Apple Health. Session definitions, timer state, and available workout metrics are shared with your paired Apple Watch to display and control the timer. This information is not sent to the developer. Apple Health and any Apple-managed synchronization follow your Apple settings. You can manage permissions and remove saved Health records in Apple Health. The Watch companion uses workout processing to keep its timer available; it does not save a duplicate workout.

Notifications And Live Activities

Yada Intervals may request permission for local timer notifications and show a Live Activity. These show session and timer information on your device. Manage their visibility in iOS Settings.

Yada Metronome

Yada Metronome plays bundled audio and stores practice settings locally. It does not access your microphone or upload your practice activity.

Retention And Deletion

Delete saved sessions or presets in the relevant app. Deleting an app removes its local data. Backups and Apple Health records are managed separately through Apple services and may remain after deleting the app.

Support

If you choose to contact support, the contact information and message you provide will be used to respond to your request. Do not include microphone recordings or Health data unless you deliberately choose to share them for support. Contact: hc19ysv9z@mozmail.com.

Changes

We will update this policy if the apps' privacy practices change. The date above identifies this version.
