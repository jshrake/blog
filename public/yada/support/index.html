Yada Support

Simple tools for everyday use. No login. No ads. No subscription.

Yada Intervals

Tap a session to begin. Tap + to create a session, then set your work and rest durations and rounds. Swipe right on a saved session to edit it. Use the timer controls to pause, resume, skip, or stop. Audio cues use your media volume. Notifications depend on your device's notification settings. To record workouts in Apple Health, choose a workout activity in the session editor and allow Health access. The Apple Watch companion requires a paired iPhone.

Yada Tuner

Tap Start tuning and allow microphone access. Pluck a single guitar string and follow Tune up, In tune, or Tune down. Tap a string to lock its target, or use Auto. Open the tuning menu to choose Standard, Drop D, DADGAD, Open D, Open G, or Half Step Down. Reference pitch is fixed at A4 = 440 Hz. If microphone access was denied, enable it in Settings. Calls may require tapping Resume before listening starts again.

Yada Metronome

Tap Start to hear the beat. Adjust tempo with the slider or +/−, or tap the tempo number to enter it. Use the meter, rhythm, and sound menus to shape your practice. Tap a beat bar to change its accent. Use the bookmark button to save a named setup. Playback continues with the screen locked; stop it from the app or Lock Screen. Audio route changes and calls stop playback, so tap Start to resume.

Requirements

All three apps require iOS 26 or later. Intervals and Metronome support iPhone and iPad. Tuner is designed for iPhone. The Intervals Watch companion requires watchOS 26 or later.
